About PK STOP

PK STOP is a trusted professional service platform based in Mumbai, providing complete assistance for PF, EPF, GST, PAN, and government-related services. With years of experience and 1000+ successful cases, we are committed to delivering reliable, transparent, and fast solutions.

Our core services include PF Withdrawal & Transfer, UAN Activation & KYC, GST Registration & GST Filing, MSME Certificate, FSSAI Registration, PAN Card Services, Import Export (IEC), Passport Services, Driving Licence Assistance, Voter ID Services, Copyright & Trademark Registration, NOC & Agreement Deed, and Gumasta Certificate Registration.

In addition to professional services, PK STOP also supplies high-quality natural resins including Gum Copal, Gum Damar, and Damar Batu. These products are widely used for incense, varnish, polish, traditional practices, and spiritual purposes.

Our mission is to provide affordable, accurate, and customer-focused solutions under one roof. We believe in transparency, professionalism, and long-term customer trust

Yes, Aadhaar linking with UAN is mandatory for online PF withdrawal.

What if my PF claim is rejected?

We help identify errors and guide you through resubmission.
